# Trend words

Updated: 2026-11-03

This file holds words, formats, sounds and hashtags that are live, by language and platform. It
is shared by every game.

**Reading it (Step 5–6).** Use an entry only while it is fresh:
- TikTok and X: 30 days from the `Seen` date. Formats there burn out within weeks, and a late
  trend reads as cringe.
- LinkedIn and Reddit: 90 days.
- Older entries move to *Stale / avoid* unless you see them alive again today.

**Updating it (every run that can search the web).** Add what you verified, with the day and the
source:
- Bump the `Seen` date of entries you saw alive.
- Move dead ones to *Stale / avoid*.
- Keep the table columns as they are: `validate_posts.dart` reads them.

Every hashtag of type `hashtag-offgame` or `event` needs a bridge sentence in the post.

Types: `slang`, `format`, `sound`, `hashtag-game`, `hashtag-offgame`, `event`, `topic`, `pattern`.

## Stale / avoid

These are dead or cringe now. Don't use them unless you see one alive again with a fresh date.

- **Vietnamese, from 2021:** khum, chằm Zn, xu cà na, còn cái nịt, tới công chuyện, tóp tóp.
- **Vietnamese, from 2024:** 8386, dịu kha, đỉnh nóc kịch trần (bay phấp phới), lemỏn, mai đẹt ti ni, in tư.
- **Vietnamese, from 2025:** To6, bốc trúng sít rịt, tuyệt đối điện ảnh, lốp trưởng, vuýp, thân chưa mà giỡn,
  +1 máy, cơm nước gì chưa người đẹp, ok mom, mời đoàn mình di chuyển lên núi.
- **Vietnamese, from 2019–2023:** ét o ét, gét gô, toang, mlem, mãi mận, cứu.
- **Vietnamese seasonal trends from early 2026:** "2026 là năm 2016 mới", "Về quê rồi sao không thấy?",
  "Dừng xe đi", Threads "Mình là…". Vietnamese slang goes "phèn" within about a month.
- **English:**
  - 6-7 (top of the 2026 banished-words list), cooked, demure.
  - "Tell me you're X without telling me", "Nobody: / Me:".
  - The brainrot set (skibidi, rizz, sigma, gyatt, Ohio). The "Great Meme Reset of 2026" was a backlash against it.
  - Aura farming and glazing are aging.
  - October-only trends: Pumpkin Head, "We Fell in Love in October".
  - "We got ___ before GTA 6" dies on 19 Nov.
- **Offensive, never use:** malfoid, foid, huzz, bop, chud, mogging, looksmaxxing, tism, clanker.
- **Sensitive, never ride:**
  - Elections (US midterms 3 Nov), politicised memes, homelandmaxxing.
  - Floods and storms in central Vietnam. Check the news on the posting day.
  - Studio layoffs (BioWare).
  - Real people's scandals.
  - Dangerous dance trends.
  - Religion, folk belief and death trends: Cây Tình Duyên, "vào chùa quét lá đa", "đám giỗ bên cồn".
  - Vietnam's 22/12 army day.
