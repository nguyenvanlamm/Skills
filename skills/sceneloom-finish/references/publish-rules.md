# Publishing package rules

## Shape of `seo.json`

```json
{
  "titles": ["3-6 alternative titles"],
  "description": "3 short paragraphs, no chapter list",
  "tags": ["aim for 15-25 tags (the import accepts 5-30)"],
  "hashtags": ["#3", "#to", "#5"],
  "pinned_comment": "a friendly comment that ends with a question",
  "thumbnail_texts": ["up to 3 texts of 2-5 words"],
  "thumbnail_scenes": [4, 7, 9]
}
```

Write everything in the video's language (`language` in `sceneloom seo facts`).

## Limits the import checks

| Field | Limit |
|---|---|
| each title | at most 100 characters (aim for under 70 so it is not cut off in search) |
| description | 80 to 4,500 characters; the chapter marks are added after it when YouTube can use them |
| tags | 5 to 30 tags, 500 characters in total including the separating commas |
| hashtags | 1 to 5, each starting with `#` and with no spaces |
| each thumbnail text | at most 5 words |

## Writing them well

- **Titles**: put your best pick first (it need not be the project's title word for word). Make the others genuinely different angles
  (the question, the emotion, the plain statement), not one title with words swapped. Put the
  subject in the first few words.
- **Description**: the first two lines are what shows before "more": say what the video is and
  why to watch, in plain words. Then a short paragraph on the story or content, then a call to
  action. Use the genre's audience note from `seo facts` for the tone.
- **Tags**: specific before general; include the genre and the main subject in the video
  language, and a few spelling variants people really type (with and without accents for
  Vietnamese). No tags for things that are not in the video.
- **Thumbnail texts**: 2-4 short words that add a question or a stake the picture does not show.
  They are drawn in capitals along the bottom of a scene picture, about 14 letters to a line;
  a longer text wraps to a second line and climbs toward the middle of the picture, where the
  main face often is. One line is safest.
- **Pinned comment**: one or two warm sentences and a real question about the video.

## Honesty rules

These protect the channel; platforms penalise misleading metadata and viewers leave.

- Promise only what the video shows. No "true story" for an invented one, no "full movie", no
  "official".
- Say the video was made with AI tools somewhere in the description when the pictures or the
  voice are AI-made (in sceneloom they are). One plain sentence is enough.
- No search volumes, rankings or view predictions: you have no data for them.
- No names of real people, shows or brands as bait when the video is not about them.
- No keyword stuffing: no tag appears twice. The unaccented spelling of a Vietnamese tag counts as
  a different tag (people search both ways), so it may sit next to the accented one.
